# Documentation update log

## 2026-09-10

* **Removal**: Retired all 17 illustrated posters. Every page on this site opened with an AI-generated illustration produced by NotebookLM — a hand-drawn collage of robots, gears and speech bubbles, typically two megabytes, carrying its content as unsearchable pixels. Twelve pages get an engineering diagram in place of the poster: a Mermaid schematic of the thing the page actually teaches, written from that page's own text and small enough to read — the RAG pipeline with the RAGAS metric that implicates each stage, the reasoning-paradigm progression from Chain-of-Thought to LATS, the Researcher–Analyst–Critic pipeline with its quality gate and revision cap, the Workflow Audit scoring pipeline, the portfolio setup checklist and its weekly loop.
* **Removal**: Seven pages lose the poster and gain nothing, each for a reason. The [Module 2 activities](modules/module-2/activities.md) page sets sketching that exact diagram as self-check prompt 2, so a figure at the top would print the answer key above the question. [Module 5 foundational concepts](modules/module-5/foundational-concepts.md) is a five-chapter survey whose structure its tables already carry. The Module 1, 2 and 3 overview pages would have repeated a diagram that now appears on their own foundational-concepts page. The [landing page](index.md) banner becomes a typographic title — the course name was previously hidden from sighted readers because the picture carried it — and the archived June 2026 draft keeps its record without being re-illustrated.
* **Update**: `docs/assets/images/` is now empty: the site serves no raster images at all, down from 42 files and 39.2 MB. All 103 originals stay in `images/` as the provenance record of what the wiki contained. Reading the 17 posters against their pages found 121 claims that appear in a poster and in no page text; the four that contradicted the page — including a Workflow Audit formula missing the `+ 1` in its denominator — are recorded on the [instructor materials](course-design/instructor-materials.md) page, along with the subject matter that existed only in the pictures.
* **Update**: Replaced 25 figures that were tables shipped as PNG screenshots with real Markdown tables. A table stored as a picture is invisible to search and to `llms.txt`, unreadable by a screen reader, impossible to copy from, and it cannot reflow on a phone — the rubric a learner is graded against was a 2096-pixel-wide image. The 25 span [Module 1 activities](modules/module-1/activities.md), [foundational concepts](modules/module-1/foundational-concepts.md) and [overview](modules/module-1/overview.md), [Module 2](modules/module-2/foundational-concepts.md), [Module 3](modules/module-3/foundational-concepts.md) and [Module 4 foundational concepts](modules/module-4/foundational-concepts.md), and the [formal learning design](course-design/learning-design.md). Four of them were not grids but coloured definition blocks — the four-pillar literacy model, the four-dimension prompt architecture, the three RAG paradigms and the Bloom's progression — and are now headed prose rather than being forced into a table.
* **Update**: Each table was transcribed from the full-resolution original in `images/`, not from the published copy, and then checked cell by cell against its image by a second reader; row and column counts were compared independently. Three were additionally verified by hand, including the graded Workflow Audit rubric. The originals stay in `images/` as the provenance record and are no longer published. `docs/assets/images/` drops from 42 files to 17, and the served image payload from 39.2 MB to 31.1 MB.
* **Update**: [Contributing](about/contributing.md) now states the convention the site follows: diagrams are Mermaid in a fence, tables are Markdown, and images are for photographs and screenshots of real interfaces.
* **Update**: Redrew the agent architecture figure on the [Module 2 foundational concepts](modules/module-2/foundational-concepts.md) page. The wiki shipped `AgentExecutor-Architecture.png`, a five-row LangChain 0.x design table naming its parts Agent (the LLM), Tool Descriptions, Tool Executor, Memory / State and Stopping Criteria. It is replaced by a Mermaid diagram of the loop `create_agent` actually runs and a table of the four components the rest of the course names, so the figure no longer disagrees with the pages that cite it. Being Mermaid, it is text in the repository rather than a binary that can silently go stale, and the course team can edit it without image tooling.
* **Removal**: The retired PNG is no longer published. It stays in `images/` as the provenance record of what the wiki contained; `scripts/optimize_images.py` dropped the served copy once no page referenced it.
* **Update**: The row-by-row crosswalk that made the old figure readable is gone with it, but the three points it carried that stand on their own are kept as prose: stopping is the rule that ends the loop rather than a fifth component; the 0.x stopping conditions — a Final Answer token, a maximum-iteration argument and a parsing-error threshold — have no equivalent in the current API; and older material names the same four components differently, which is still where a learner meets both vocabularies at once. No quiz question or reading guide referenced the figure, so no assessment item changed.

## 2026-09-09

* **Update**: Moved the course onto current LangChain. LangChain 1.0 removed `AgentExecutor`, `create_react_agent`, `create_tool_calling_agent`, `initialize_agent`, `langchain.chains`, `langchain.memory`, `langchain.prompts` and `langchain.tools.Tool` from the `langchain` package with no deprecation shim, so those imports now fail with `AttributeError` or `ModuleNotFoundError` rather than a warning. The [Module 2 lab](modules/module-2/lab-notebook.md) was built entirely on `AgentExecutor` and installed LangChain unpinned, so it raised `ImportError` on its agent-building cell for every learner; it is rewritten on `create_agent`, which is what the module's own [foundational concepts](modules/module-2/foundational-concepts.md) page already documented. The [Module 2 resources](modules/module-2/resources.md) page carried two LangChain 0.x code samples with the same problem; both are rewritten. Its conceptual model of an agent runtime is framework-agnostic and is unchanged.
* **Update**: The lab's four-step shape, its student extension points, its self-checks and its log entries are preserved. What changed is how the reasoning loop is observed: `create_agent` returns a LangGraph graph rather than an `AgentExecutor`, so there is no `verbose=True` text trace printing `Thought:`, `Action:` and `Observation:`. A `show_trace` helper streams the graph instead and prints the same loop as structured data, and Step 1 now explains why the old labels are gone. Two sunset dependencies were dropped: `langchain-experimental`, which the lab imported but never installed, and `langchain-community`, whose DuckDuckGo wrapper now needs the renamed `ddgs` package. Both tools are defined in the notebook instead.
* **Update**: Pinned every notebook to the same current stack: LangChain 1.4.0, LangGraph 1.2.11 and LangSmith 0.12.4. Modules 3 and 5 had drifted to different LangChain patch versions, and Module 2 had no pin at all. Module 5's optional live-search cell installed the renamed `duckduckgo-search` package and never installed the library it imported, so it failed twice if a learner uncommented it. Its LangSmith setup now uses the documented `LANGSMITH_*` variables and clears the older `LANGCHAIN_*` ones first, because the older names take precedence and the value is matched against the literal lowercase string `true`; either trap turns tracing off silently and leaves an empty project with no error.
* **Update**: The four lab pages regained the `last_modified` date in their `sources` entry. `scripts/render_notebooks.py` was still looking for the notebook clone under `.cache/`, which `zensical build --clean` deletes because it is Zensical's own build cache; it now reads `.sources/` like the migration pipeline does, so it can read the notebooks' git history again.
* **Update**: Revised the assessment content that examined the removed LangChain APIs, closing the gap recorded below. 99 changes across the [Module 2 chapter quizzes](modules/module-2/chapter-quizzes.md) and [reading guides](modules/module-2/reading-guides.md), the [Module 3 chapter quizzes](modules/module-3/chapter-quizzes.md), [reading guides](modules/module-3/reading-guides.md) and [activities](modules/module-3/activities.md), and the [Module 2 foundational concepts](modules/module-2/foundational-concepts.md) lesson the quizzes cite by name. Both quiz pages still hold 25 questions and 25 answer keys, no correct answer moved to a different letter, and no question was added or dropped. Questions that asked a learner to reason about a mechanism still do rather than becoming vocabulary recall, and distractors were rewritten to stay plausible against the current API. This is what the [July 2026 course review](course-design/course-review-2026-07.md) asked for when it flagged the `AgentExecutor` dependency.
* **Update**: Two conventions now hold across those pages. One component vocabulary, the four names already used on the [Module 2 resources](modules/module-2/resources.md) page: LLM Backbone, Tool Registry, Action Executor and Memory Module. The old list's fifth member, the output parser, is genuinely gone because a tool-calling model returns a structured call rather than text to parse, and that disappearance is now taught rather than papered over. And one trace format: every trace a learner is shown is what the lab's `show_trace` helper prints, so `TOOL CALL:`, `ARGUMENTS:`, `OBSERVATION:` and `[final] ANSWER:` rather than the `Thought:` and `Action:` labels a current agent never emits. The `AgentExecutor` architecture figure is kept and captioned as the LangChain 0.x table it is, with a row-by-row crosswalk onto the four names; its alt text, which wrongly described a loop diagram, now describes the table that is actually there.
* **Known gap**: Eight chapter-quiz questions across Modules 1, 2 and 4 name the correct answer and explain only two of the three distractors, leaving one option with no feedback. All eight are inherited from the wiki unchanged, and a review of every key on all five quiz pages found no other gap: each key resolves to a stated option and each ✅ and ❌ marks the letter it should. Because the quizzes are self-evaluating, a learner who picked the unexplained option learns the right answer but not why theirs was wrong. Listed question by question on the [instructor materials](course-design/instructor-materials.md) page.
* **Known gap**: The Module 3 hands-on project remains blocked on an author's decision. The [activities page](modules/module-3/activities.md) describes a four-run parameter experiment with a RAGAS write-up and says the instructions are embedded in the lab notebook; the notebook has no such cells. Because that project is the primary module grade, a learner cannot complete the graded work as described. Whether the notebook gains cells or the page loses a promise is a curriculum decision, so it is recorded on the [instructor materials](course-design/instructor-materials.md) page rather than guessed at here.

The header logo is the CARC starburst and the favicon is the UNM mark; the palette moves from Arizona Blue and Red to UNM Cherry `#BA0C2F` and Turquoise `#007A86` with CARC's cherry-to-turquoise header gradient.
