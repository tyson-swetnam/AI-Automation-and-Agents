# Documentation update log

## 2026-09-09

* **Update**: Rebranded the site to the University of New Mexico. The header logo is the CARC starburst and the favicon is the UNM mark; the palette moves from Arizona Blue and Red to UNM Cherry `#BA0C2F` and Turquoise `#007A86` with CARC's cherry-to-turquoise header gradient. Institutional identity now reads University of New Mexico across the site description, the [syllabus](start-here/syllabus.md), [How this course works](start-here/how-this-course-works.md), the landing page, `llms.txt`, `robots.txt`, the README and the contributor contract. **Provenance is deliberately unchanged**: every page's `sources` entries, wiki URLs, provenance footers, the [wiki crosswalk](about/wiki-crosswalk.md), the entries below and the origin section of the [license page](about/license-and-attribution.md) still record that the course was written by the AI2S team at the University of Arizona. Branding says who publishes the site today; provenance says who wrote the material, and rewriting it would be false.
* **Update**: Scoped the site explicitly as **non-credit professional development** offered by CARC. The syllabus now states this in a Credit row and in the completion section, lists rolling enrollment, sets the level to Intermediate, and names the instructor of record. A separate for-credit version of the course, with its own assessments, quizzes, tests and final exams, is planned at the UNM Department of Computer Science and is not published here. Dropped the AI Education Series professional badge and the "Course 2 of the series" framing from the syllabus, [How this course works](start-here/how-this-course-works.md) and the [instructor materials](course-design/instructor-materials.md): the series is a University of Arizona program, and rebranding those sentences to UNM would have described a Course 1 and a badge that do not exist here.
* **Creation**: The downloadable syllabus PDF is now generated from [the syllabus page](start-here/syllabus.md) by `scripts/build_syllabus_pdf.py`, so the two cannot drift; CI fails when the committed PDF no longer matches the page. It replaces the University of Arizona syllabus v2.0 as the linked download. That earlier PDF stays in the repository as the source document and is still linked from the syllabus page.
* **Update**: Resolved the licensing conflict recorded below. The course content is now published under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/){target=_blank}, replacing both the wiki footer's CC BY-NC-SA notice and the repository's CC0 `LICENSE` file. CC BY is approved for Free Cultural Works and meets the Open Definition, so the course qualifies as an open educational resource under the strict reading; a NonCommercial clause would have blocked the workforce and paid-certificate reuse the course is written for. Copyright is **The Regents of the University of New Mexico, Center for Advanced Research Computing**. The course was developed at the Arizona Institute for Artificial Intelligence and the Office of Responsible AI at the University of Arizona and released there under the [CC0 1.0 public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/){target=_blank}, which is what allows this site to be licensed CC BY; that original stays public domain and available upstream. CC0 imposes no conditions, so the credit to Carlos Lizárraga-Celaya, Michelle Yung, Michele Cosi and Tyson Swetnam recorded on the license page is scholarly practice rather than a license requirement. The site's own source code (the `scripts/` pipeline, the site configuration and the stylesheet) is licensed separately under the **BSD 3-Clause License** in `LICENSE-CODE`, copyright UNM Regents, matching the notice CARC uses on its documentation; Creative Commons licenses are not intended for software. Updated the `LICENSE` file, the site footer, the landing page badge, the [license and attribution](about/license-and-attribution.md) page, the [instructor materials](course-design/instructor-materials.md) page, and the README. Third-party material and both universities' marks remain outside these licenses.
* **Update**: The course owners confirmed that quiz answer keys stay **public but collapsed**: the quizzes are written to be self-evaluating. The [instructor materials](course-design/instructor-materials.md) page now records this as a decision rather than an open question, and notes that the keys are consequently visible in `llms-full.txt` and in each page's Markdown mirror.

## 2026-09-08

* **Initialization**: Created this site as an [Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md){target=_blank} knowledge bundle built with [Zensical](https://zensical.org){target=_blank}, following the pattern of the [UNM CARC documentation](https://carc.unm.edu/docs/){target=_blank}. Every content page carries frontmatter with `type`, provenance (`generated`, `sources`, `authorship`) and lifecycle (`status`, `stale_after`); section `index.md` files are directory listings; this file is the dated change log.
* **Migration**: Migrated the 42 pages of the [AI Automation and Agents v2 GitHub wiki](https://github.com/UA-AI2S/AI-Automation-and-Agents-v2/wiki){target=_blank} (commit `fcc9835d10d3`) with `scripts/migrate_wiki.py`: 38 wiki files became 43 site pages (the five "Addendum" pages were each split into a reading-guides page and a chapter-quizzes page). Raw HTML heroes became responsive images, GitHub alerts and one-column callout tables became admonitions, exposed answer keys became collapsed `??? success` blocks, letter and roman heading prefixes were removed, wiki links were rewritten to relative site links, and each page's `Created/Updated` footer moved into the frontmatter. Every migrated page is **unverified** pending instructor review; see the [wiki-to-site crosswalk](about/wiki-crosswalk.md) for the full mapping.
* **Creation**: Wrote the landing page, [Start here](start-here/index.md) (how the course works, syllabus, labs and notebooks), the [Module 1 concept quiz](modules/module-1/concept-quiz.md) and [diagnostic survey](modules/module-1/diagnostic-survey.md) pages that embed the interactive HTML activities, the [instructor materials](course-design/instructor-materials.md) page, and the [About](about/index.md) section (license and attribution, contributing, for AI agents, crosswalk). Rendered the four learner lab notebooks as read-only pages with Open-in-Colab buttons (`scripts/render_notebooks.py`).
* **Creation**: Added the machine-readable surface: `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`, a Markdown mirror of every page at its URL plus `index.md`, `okf:*` meta tags, and `robots.txt` (`scripts/gen_llms_txt.py`, `scripts/postbuild_agent_surface.py`).
