# Guest lectures

Standalone lectures the course team gives to other programs, each published with interactive slides, a PDF and numbered references.

* [Guest Lecture: Agentic AI Loops](agentic-ai-loops.md) - A 30-minute overview of agentic AI for UNM's Ethical AI for Autonomous Systems course, for a mixed technical and non-technical class: what it is, how it differs from predictive models and language models, and how sequence, selection and iteration explain the agent loop.
* [Guest Lecture: Say What You Mean](say-what-you-mean.md) - A 30-minute guest lecture on AI ethics and the resources CARC is building, for UNM's CS 491/591 Introduction to HCI: a primer on metadata, knowledge graphs, ML, RL and agents, vibe coding and voice prompts, and documentation written for AI agents.
* [Guest Lecture: Agentic AI for Engineering Research](agentic-ai-for-engineering-research.md) - A 90-minute guest lecture and hands-on for UNM's Introduction to Reliability Engineering: what AI agents are and how to build and apply them across a research project on free tools, AI watermarks and unreliable AI-text detectors, and an agent that drafts a Mars hazard assessment.
