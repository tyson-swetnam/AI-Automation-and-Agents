## Question 1

In the BDI (Belief-Desire-Intention) framework, which term specifically describes the agent's internal representation of the state of its environment, which may be incomplete or incorrect?

- **A.** Intention
- **B.** Belief
- **C.** Desire
D. Obligation

??? success "Answer and rationale"

    **Answer: B**

    - Intentions are desires the agent has committed to act upon, representing a plan rather than a model of the environment.
    - Beliefs represent the information attitudes of the agent, constituting its internal model of the world which is subject to change upon new perceptions.
    - Desires represent pro-attitudes of states of affairs the agent wishes to bring about, rather than its knowledge of the current state.
    - Obligations are social or contractual constraints on behavior, not the agent's internal knowledge base.
