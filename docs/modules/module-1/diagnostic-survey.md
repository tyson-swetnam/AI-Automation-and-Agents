# Module 1 Diagnostic Knowledge Survey

Before you write a single prompt, run a single tool, or map a single workflow,
the course wants an accurate picture of three constructs that every later
module takes for granted: the **agent loop**, the **automation paradigm
taxonomy**, and the difference between an **AI agent** and a chatbot, script
or pipeline. This 12-question survey captures your current mental models of
them. It is **ungraded** and takes about **10 minutes**.

## When and how to take it

- **Complete it before opening any readings.** Its purpose is to record what
  you think *now*, which is the baseline the course uses to measure growth.
  Do not look up answers.
- **Take it in the LMS.** Your responses there are visible to the instructor
  before Module 2 begins, and immediately after submitting you receive an
  anonymised cohort summary comparing your prior-experience profile with the
  rest of the class. Read that feedback carefully: learners consistently
  misjudge their relative experience before seeing peer data, and it
  calibrates how much technical detail will be assumed rather than taught.
- **Write something real in the last question.** The open-ended prompt -
  *"Name one repetitive task at work or school that you wish you could hand
  off"* - seeds the most important deliverable in the course, the
  [Workflow Audit Project](activities.md). The quality of that project
  depends on choosing genuine workflows from your own practice, not generic
  examples; this is your first chance to start identifying them.

## What it asks

| Section | Questions | Covers |
| :-- | :--: | :-- |
| 1 - Prior experience with automation tools | 1-3 | Tools and platforms you have used; how much you have automated before |
| 2 - Current working definition of "AI agent" | 4-7 | Your own definition, and how you currently distinguish agents from chatbots, scripts and pipelines |
| 3 - Comfort level with Python | 8-11 | Self-rated comfort and experience with Python and notebooks (1-5 scales and checklists) |
| 4 - Open-ended reflection | 12 | The repetitive task you would hand off |
