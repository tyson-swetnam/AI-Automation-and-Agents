# Course design

Instructor-facing design documents: the formal learning design, development plan, instructor materials, and the July 2026 course review.

* [Formal Learning Design](learning-design.md) - Course-level instructional design document mapping the standard per-module activity structure to Bloom's Taxonomy and defining the general course competencies and the module-specific skills for Modules 1-5.
* [Course Development Plan](development-plan.md) - Pointer to the Google Sheets milestone tracker used to plan and track development of the course. *(draft)*
* [Instructor materials](instructor-materials.md) - Where the instructor solution notebooks live, the course's answer-key policy, LMS gating notes for quizzes and formative items, and how an instructor marks a page as verified.
* [Course Review Analysis (July 2026)](course-review-2026-07.md) - Internal pedagogical review of the five-module course written from a graduate student's perspective, flagging overview/content mismatches, prerequisite escalation, and writing-load imbalance as of July 2026. *(archived)*
